How UPI-Shield Works
Four interconnected phases that together form a complete fraud intelligence system.
⚙️
01
Feature Engineering
Extracts 30+ risk signals from raw transaction data including velocity windows, amount z-scores, geo-velocity, temporal patterns and device fingerprints.

Transformer + GNN
BERT-style encoder models 50-transaction sequences per user. GraphSAGE + GAT detects fraud rings via cosine-similarity community detection across the user-merchant-device graph.
